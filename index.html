The problem

Doing nothing is the most expensive option on the table.

Patreon's forced migration has four traps built into it. Each one is documented in Patreon's own Help Center — and each one lands on creators who wait.

Trap 01 · Paused billing

Auto-switched pages stop getting paid

$0 collected while paused

If you don't switch yourself, Patreon auto-switches you on November 1 — and your billing is paused until you configure your new prices. Paused months are never billed later. That revenue isn't delayed. It's gone.

Trap 02 · Peak-month default

Your tiers get repriced off your single busiest month

$5 → $15 per month, by default

The auto-switch sets your monthly price using a default multiplier equal to your highest paid-post month of the last 3 months. Patreon's own example: a $5 per-creation tier with up to 3 paid posts in a month becomes a $15/month tier — whether your members ever actually paid $15 or not. Switch yourself and you can use a suggested multiplier based on your 3-month average instead.

Trap 03 · Help desk closed

Patreon's free 1:1 switching help is already gone

Oct 15 support closed, 2026

Patreon's free one-to-one switching support closed on October 15, 2026. From here to the deadline, you're on your own with the pricing math, the member caps, and the announcement — or you hand it to someone who does this all day.

Trap 04 · Caps & iOS math

Limits and Apple fees quietly rewrite your margins

+42.86% Patreon's iOS maintain-earnings price

Members' monthly limits are honored through the switch — good — but they have to be mapped correctly or pledges misprice. And on iOS, in-app purchases carry Apple's 30% fee: Patreon's price that maintains your earnings is 42.86% higher than your web price. Get either wrong and you either overcharge your fans or eat the fee yourself.

FAQ

Straight answers, from Patreon's own rules.

What exactly happens if I do nothing?
On November 1, 2026, Patreon auto-switches your page to subscription billing. Your billing is paused until you configure your new prices — and paused months are never billed later. When pricing does get set under the default, your tiers are multiplied by your highest paid-post month of the last 3 months, not your average. If your busiest month had 3 paid posts, a $5 tier becomes $15/month.
What's the actual deadline?
To switch on your own terms, you must complete the move to subscription billing by October 31, 2026, at 11:59 pm PT. After that, the auto-switch takes over on November 1.
Can I switch back if I don't like the new prices?
No. The switch to subscription billing is permanent. That's the core reason to get the pricing right before you move — and why TierSwitch models every tier and gets your approval before executing.
What happens to members who set monthly limits?
Their limits are honored through the switch. TierSwitch maps and verifies each capped membership against its new monthly price so nobody is charged above the limit they set.
Why is the iOS price 42.86% higher?
Purchases made inside Patreon's iOS app carry Apple's 30% fee. To end up with the same earnings as a web purchase, the iOS price has to be grossed up — Patreon's maintain-earnings figure is +42.86% over the web price. We configure this deliberately, tier by tier, instead of letting it surprise you or your members.
Can't I just ask Patreon for help switching?
Not anymore. Patreon's free 1:1 switching support closed on October 15, 2026. The Help Center documentation remains, but the human guidance is gone — which is the gap TierSwitch fills.
I'm on first-of-the-month billing, not per-creation. Does this apply to me?
Yes. The mandate covers every legacy billing model — per-creation and first-of-the-month alike. All of it moves to subscription billing by November 1, 2026.
How does the No-Pause Promise work?
Buy a Switch Pack before the deadline and we deliver your completed migration before November 1, 2026 — prices configured, billing live. If we miss that, the Switch Pack is free. Sentinel is a separate monthly service and isn't part of the guarantee.
What do you need from me to start?
